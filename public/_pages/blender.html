Frequently asked questions

How do you blend two hex colors?

Each RGB channel is mixed with linear interpolation: new = A × (1 − t) + B × t, where t is the mix ratio from 0 to 1.

What does a 50% mix of two colors look like?

A 50% mix produces the average of the two RGB values, which is the midpoint of the interpolation ramp.
